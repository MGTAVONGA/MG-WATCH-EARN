# MG TECH Terms of Use — Draft

**Status:** Draft for legal review; not yet a binding, launch-ready legal document.

MG TECH is an educational defensive-cybersecurity service. Use it only for learning, protecting systems you own, or systems for which you have explicit written permission. Do not attempt unauthorised access, disruption, credential theft, malware deployment, evasion, or testing of third-party systems. Do not upload information you do not have permission to share.

Training scenarios are fictional and do not certify that a real system is secure. The service is provided as an educational tool; users remain responsible for validating advice and complying with applicable law. The operator must add its legal identity, jurisdiction, contact details, acceptable-use enforcement process, age requirements, liability terms, dispute process and consumer/subscription terms before public launch.
